# Concept

Back: [[00 - Home]]

## In one sentence
Some nights the moon rises red; during that blood moon, spectacular events happen around the players, and dawn burns them away.

## Bastien's direction (2026-11-14)
- He loved the "cataclysms" and "crystal corruption" ideas and asked for a **blood moon mod** where "during the blood moon we can do lots of events, like the TikToks".
- Rendering first: own GPU passes, real models, HD textures; never plain code boxes as a final look.

## Rules carried from his earlier feedback (Cagebound, 2026-11-13)
- **You must see it**: nothing hidden underground or off-screen.
- **Nothing moves without a visible reason** (no items flying around unexplained).
- **Logical in Minecraft**.
- **Don't get stuck on one shape**: explore truly different options before refining one.
- **Preview first**: an animated page with candidates before coding a look.
- **No ugly vanilla stand-ins** (a wooden sign for a tech display was turned down; on 2026-11-14 the nether portal's noise as a drone too: sounds must be our own).

## The night
1. **Rise**: the moon turns red and swells, sky gradient and fog go crimson, grass and leaves darken (live biome colours), a heartbeat starts.
2. **Peak**: events, picked by the scheduler, around each player.
3. **Dawn**: the sun rises with a golden wave that burns the crimson away; what was harvested stays.
