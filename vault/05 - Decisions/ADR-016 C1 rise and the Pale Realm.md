# ADR-016: The rise is C1, and the sky breaks onto the Pale Realm

## Context
- Staging C (The Abyss) was picked, then judged "cool, not masterclass" in game. Three climax candidates followed in `preview/abyss-climax.html`; Bastien picked **C1 · Shell of Light**, then reshaped it over many rounds (see [[Blood moon rise]] from § Bastien's pick).
- Bastien's long-term goal: players will be able to **go into the world seen behind the sky**. So it must be a real place, not a backdrop.

## Decision
- **The break**: one crack system only. Real fissures fixed in the sky, born at the moon's edge or off another crack on a heartbeat, tips running fast then slowing; a main fault crossing the sky in stages; at the climax a burst of cracks. The fissures themselves open into lenses onto the other world. No moon-centred web.
- **The eye**: C1's (strands of flesh, a third eyelid, lock-on).
- **The other world**: the **Pale Realm**, seen as its sky. Bone-white fog, a black sun with a thin bright ring, floating black shards, ash; no ground in sight (thick fog far below). Far serpent dragons only (none close). One walking presence: a **black smoky shadow with two bright eyes**, feet lost in the fog (a future mob, kept vague so it can be modelled freely).
- **Its main structure**: **S1 · the Spire**, a colossal black needle tower with ledges and side spikes, broken rings turning around it, a pale light at the tip. It is likely the heart of the zone players will reach.
- Kept as an alternative: W1 · Storm Realm (Bastien liked it too).

## Consequences
- + A clear identity: crimson sky, white world behind it, black shapes.
- + The landmarks seen through the sky (Spire, shadow, black sun) become real things of a future dimension.
- − The sky pass grows: a segment buffer for the fissures (filled by the pure core), a second world function, the god-ray pass. Port step by step and keep each part switchable.
- ❓ Whether a native mod can add a dimension at the pin is unknown: [[Open questions]].
