# Glossary

- **Blood moon**: a night when the moon rises red and events can happen. Phases: rise, peak, dawn.
- **Event**: one spectacular thing that happens during a blood moon (a meteor, a sky crack, a rift…), see [[Events]].
- **Scheduler**: the pure rule that picks which events happen when, so each night differs.
- **Crimson crystal**: what blood meteors plant; it spreads, glows, can be harvested and burns away at dawn.
- **GPU pass**: drawing of our own after the world, through `RenderHooks::world_drawn` ([[Rendering toolkit]]).
- **Glue**: the thin hook layer between Horizon and the pure crate.
- **Structure**: a built place the world generates, small, medium or immense ([[Structures]]).
- **Template**: a vanilla-format NBT file holding one piece of a structure (`data/crimson_eclipse/structure/`).
- **Anchor**: one of our blocks placed inside a structure whose block entity brings it to life ([[How a structure is made]]).
- **Awakening**: a structure coming alive under the blood moon.
