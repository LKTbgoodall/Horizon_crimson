# Vision and goals

## In one sentence
On blood moon nights the moon rises red and the world goes through spectacular events, the kind that make people stop scrolling.

## Why
- Bastien saw TikTok videos of Horizon mods (giant glowing crystals with light beams and spreading corrupted terrain; the sky cracking like glass with an eye behind it) and wants that level of spectacle.
- On Cagebound he turned down a look made of code boxes with vanilla textures: the effort goes into rendering first.
- A blood moon is logical in Minecraft (a night, the moon, monsters) and is a natural stage for many events.

## Goals
- 🔴 The rise of the blood moon is a show on its own: the moon turns red and grows, the sky and fog turn crimson, the grass darkens, a heartbeat starts.
- 🔴 A first signature event done at "masterclass" level (see [[Events]]).
- 🟠 Several events that can happen during a blood moon, chained so each night differs.
- 🟠 Something to gain: crimson crystals and what they make, so players want the blood moon to come.
- 🟡 Public release on playhorizon.dev.

## Non-goals
- No gore for its own sake: dramatic, not disgusting.
- Nothing that destroys a player's base for good (corruption can be purified, dawn burns it back).
- Not a recreation of another game's blood moon or moon (no copied look, face or mechanics).
