- **First look in game** (Bastien built 0.1.0, screenshots): the sky pass draws (web of cracks, torn sky on space, corona, the eye, clouds passing dark in front). Four problems, fixed in **0.1.1**:
  1. *No sound*: none was written. Now `crimson-core::sound` gives cues per client tick (omen, drone, heartbeat on the rise's own beat, silence, crack) and `client.rs` plays them in the `tick` hook with `play_local_sound` and `muffle_sounds`, using vanilla sound events by name, pitched down. Stand-ins: no Ogg encoder on this PC.
  2. *The pale moon is ugly and sits above the real one*: the moon's angle is eased by the game's day timeline, not linear (`sky::day_turn`, a cubic Bézier). At 13000 the real moon is 3.5° up, not 15°. The night now starts 400 ticks later (omens 12200, eclipse 13400 to 14600, moon 10° to 30° up). The moon is amber with seas and craters.
  3. *White shapes over the moon and the eye*: the float hash (`fract` of a big product) gave two different values for the same Voronoi cell at two places of the shader, so a cell's own point was taken for a neighbour and the edge distance went negative across the cell. Hashes now work on the bits (`bitcast`, PCG), the cell's own point is skipped by index, and the distance is clamped.
  4. *A hard cut between our sky and the rest*: sky pixels under the horizon were one flat colour. The sky now sinks into the fog's colour around and under the horizon; bright parts are compressed (`1 - exp(-c * 1.2)`) since there is no bloom yet.

## 🧠 Learned / found
- **Never compute the same pseudo-random thing twice in a shader and compare** (seen in game twice, 0.1.0 and 0.1.3): one-pass algorithms on integer cells only.
- A content mod with any `assets/` or `data/` file must list `capabilities = ["registries"]` (`horizon-mods/src/content.rs`).
- **Clouds** (read at `9f42064`): no hook point; `FogData { color, environmental_start/end, render_distance_start/end, sky_end, cloud_end }` in `render_fog` (ends must stay nonnegative and finite or the whole request is dropped); the cloud shader multiplies alpha by `1 - linear_fog(distance, 0, cloud_end)` and discards under 0.01; `visual/cloud_height` defaults to 192.33.
- **Seen in game** (0.1.0, `latest.log`): `sky pass on Bgra8Unorm (Bgra8Unorm texture), 2560x1369, depth 2560x1369, frame copy: true`. Clouds write depth.
- **The sky's turn is eased**: `data/minecraft/timeline/day.json` of game 26.3 (read in the launcher's installed version, not copied): `visual/moon_angle` goes 180 → 540 from tick 6000 to the next, `sun_angle` and `star_angle` 0 → 360, all with `ease: cubic_bezier [0.362, 0.241, 0.638, 0.759]`. A data pack can change it; no hook or host method gives the angle.
- **A float hash is not stable inside one shader** on the GPU (FMA contraction differs from one call site to another): never compare two evaluations of `fract(big product)`. Use integer hashes.
- Client sound API read at `9f42064`: `Hooks::tick` runs in every `Game` (`host.window()` is true for the window's), `host.player() -> Option<NativePlayer { x, y, z, yaw, pitch, dimension }>`, `host.play_local_sound(event, at, volume, pitch, delay_ticks 0..=1200) -> bool` (client only, vanilla or mod events), `host.muffle_sounds(amount, lowpass, ticks, exempt)`. Type-checked, not heard yet.
- Render API read at `9f42064` (`native_render.rs`, its `generated.rs`, wgpu 30.0.1): `Hooks::render(&self) -> Option<&dyn RenderHooks>`; `RenderHooks { world_drawn, render_needs, hand_drawn, world_warp }`, no host in any of them; `WorldDrawn.view_proj` is a glam `Mat4` (`.inverse().to_cols_array()` needs no glam import); wgpu comes from `horizon_client::mod_api::wgpu`. The vanilla moon is a quad of half-size 20 at distance 100 along `(-sin a, cos a, 0)` with `a = sky.moon_angle` (`frame_geometry.rs`), its phase `(day_time / 24000) % 8`.
- API read at `9f42064` for the glue (generated `mod_api_generated*.rs`): `Host::level(dim) -> Option<LevelRef>` (server only, `Copy`), `level_day_time`, `level_set_day_time(level, time) -> bool`, client `Host::day_time()`; `register_commands` takes `NativeCommand::op("crimson start", 2)`, `perform_command` gets `event.command` without the slash and answers with `host.feedback(Text)`; `ComputeFogColorEvent { partial, camera, fog_type: horizon_client::fog::FogType, rgb: &mut [f32; 3] }`; `CameraShakeEvent { partial, shake: &mut NativeShake { pitch, yaw, roll, offset } }`.
- Read at the pin `9f42064` (Cagebound's `ext/src-9f42064`; this repo has no `ext/` yet): `native-hooks.json` has **no moon, sky or star hook** (only `compute_fog_color`, `render_fog`, `day_time`, `weather`). The sky must be painted by our `world_drawn` pass where depth is 0. `world_warp` also accepts a `world_fragment` (after a `// [fragments]` line) that paints every terrain pixel. → [[Horizon hooks and API]] § A8, [[Open questions]] #6.

## The dark arc explained → 0.3.2
- Bastien's second screenshot and answers: the line does not move, it is only in the holes; and "it may break under the moon".
- The arc is a circle of constant elevation, at the height of the Spire's tip (0.73 in the Pale Realm's own coordinates), all round. Cause (deduced, not reproduced; the cloud-depth guess was wrong): in `spire()`, above the tip `k` is exactly 0 and the width used `pow(k, 1.3)`. On his GPU that is not a number; `max(NaN, x)` returns `x`, so the distance became `lp.y - hgt` and the silhouette drew a band where that is under 0.7. A Python copy of the function with exact maths shows nothing there, which is what pointed at the GPU.
- **Learned: never take a fractional power of a value that can be exactly 0 in a shader** (integer powers were fine on the same GPU: the sun's rays use `pow(max(0, sin), 20)`). Guarded: the Spire's width, the sky gradient, the eye's lid shape; the iris' `pow(x, 2)` with a negative base is now `x * x`.
- `shell.rs`: `CORE` (0.25 rad): every slab nearer the impact falls at the climax, hole floor permitting; these small slabs are not counted in `MIN_HOLES` nor heard. Cracks fade in from 0.05 to 0.14 rad.
- Checks: fmt, clippy, tests, shader, glue. Version 0.3.2. **Not seen in game; the line's fix is to confirm.**

## A pale rectangle under the rising moon → 0.3.4
- Bastien's screenshot (moonrise, 0.3.3): our amber moon is cut on a hard horizontal line; under it, in a blue band, a paler rectangle about 0.05 rad half-wide, centred under the moon; trees in front.
- Reading (not reproduced): the band is far water. It has depth, so `is_sky` is false: the sky pass neither paints our moon nor replaces the sky there, hence the hard cut. The rectangle is the vanilla moon's sprite, under the horizon, showing through the translucent water where nothing is loaded behind it. There is no hook to turn the vanilla moon off ([[Horizon hooks and API]]).
- Fix: `unmoon` (WGSL, `fs_copy` only). A land pixel inside the sprite's square and brighter than the frame just left and right of the square takes the brighter of those two samples; the horizontal step comes from `dpdx` of the pixel's place around the moon. Darker things in front (trees) are left alone. Known limits: a bright thing in front of the square with dark things on both sides would be darkened; nothing is done without a frame copy.
- Checks: shader valid (868 lines), glue clean. Version 0.3.4. **Not seen in game.**
