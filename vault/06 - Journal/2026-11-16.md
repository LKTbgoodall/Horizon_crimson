## ✅ Done
- Shared structure preview engine `preview/lib/` ([[ADR-020 Structure previews in real blocks]]):
  - `blocks.js`: about 550 blocks with vanilla ids and state strings; shapes from vanilla pixel sizes (stairs with auto corner shapes, slabs, fences / walls / panes / iron bars that connect, doors, trapdoors, gates, carpets, lanterns, chains, candles 1 to 4 with flames, torches, chest with its lid, bell, wall banners, vines, plants, water, lava); ours: crimson crystal and cluster, eclipse glass, eclipse eye, moon lens, rune stone, blood water, the armillary and vigil clock anchors.
  - `world.js`: the grid (`W.set` refuses off-grid positions), building kit (collapse, weather, vines, terrain with plants, spruce, dead tree, stepped pyramid roofs, size), Minecraft light (sky and block levels, smooth lighting, corner shading, warm and red light with awakening delays), mesher with face culling.
  - `textures.js`: vanilla textures by name from the player's jar (in-page reader kept in IndexedDB, or `preview/vanilla/textures.js`), painted stand-ins otherwise, our textures painted in code.
  - `viewer.js`: the renderer of the previous previews (sky, HDR, bloom, beams, particles, moving groups) plus hover to read a block's id and position, double-click to centre, right-drag to move, a layer cut slider.
- `preview/block-palette.html`: every block, id on hover.
- `preview/abandoned-building.html` rebuilt in real blocks, all on the grid: pews are stairs, the altar candles are candle blocks that light one by one, the chest and the bell are blocks (the lid and the bell are moving parts), banners, a fallen door of trapdoors, crosses and headstones of walls, iron bar fences and gate, glass panes with a candle behind each manor window, stair-edged roofs, vines as vine blocks. Checked in the browser: day and blood moon of A, B, C, hover, the grid refusal.
