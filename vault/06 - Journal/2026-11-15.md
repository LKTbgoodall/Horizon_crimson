## 🧠 Learned / found
- A content mod ships vanilla-format structures (templates, jigsaw pools, structure sets), plus Horizon's `horizon:add_to`, `horizon:enhanced_terrain_adaptation`, `horizon:start_checks`.
- A world's data pack worldgen does not run: structures must be in the mod.
- `structure_template` (build block by block) and `chunk_generate` are ⏳ not numbered at `9f42064`.
- Block entity drawing stops at 64 blocks: far motion of giant structures needs a GPU pass.
