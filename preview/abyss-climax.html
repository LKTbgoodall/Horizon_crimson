What changes from C, and how it renders

All three keep C's minute (swelling moon, spiral void, accretion corona, spiral aurora, the hush). Only the sky's breaking and the eye change.

C1 · Shell of LightThe sky is a shell with blinding light behind it. Cracks run out from the moon like impact glass, white-hot at their tip then cooling to red. At the climax the shards near the moon blow out and light pours through: god rays fall across the sky and through the trees. The eye opens slowly, held by strands of flesh that stretch and snap, then snaps wide; a third eyelid sweeps across it, then the pupil locks onto you.
C2 · Glass AbyssThe sky is glass. Each shard bends the sky behind it, catches glints, and the cracks split light like a prism. At the climax the shards fall away in a wave from the moon and leave a hole onto deep space with a galaxy spiralling into the moon. The eye has no lid: the void that ate the moon was a pupil dilated in the dark. At the flash it contracts into a slit and the iris ignites, hottest where it was just uncovered.
C3 · The Sky OpensOne rift grows across the whole sky through the eclipse, in steps on the heartbeat, with cracks branching off it. At the climax it lights up from horizon to horizon, then the sky itself opens like an eyelid, 130° wide. The moon becomes the pupil of an eye the size of the sky, and it follows you across it.

In Horizon

The sky, the cracks, the holes, the eye: the same single world_drawn pass as 0.1.5 (WGSL port of this shader). The crack network is log-polar (conformal) one-pass Voronoi on integer cells, so nothing is computed twice.

God rays: one extra full-screen pass in the same hook, a 48-tap radial blur toward the moon on a quarter-size bright copy. Needs our own intermediate texture (we already copy the frame). Not tried in game yet (❓).

The glow itself is the bloom still on the list; until it exists in game, bright parts are compressed as in 0.1.1.
