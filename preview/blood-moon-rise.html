How each thing is rendered in Horizon

Checked against the pin 9f42064 (native_render.rs, docs/native-mods.md). Read in source, not yet seen in game unless noted.

Moon, eclipse shadow, moon cracks, flame corona, blood aurora, sky cracks, the eye, stars going outOne full-screen fragment shader in RenderHooks::world_drawn, written only where the world depth is 0 (the sky; render_needs.world_depth). It paints over the vanilla moon and stars: there is no moon hook at the pin. This page's GLSL ports to WGSL almost line for line.
Glow / bloomSame hook: copy the colour target out, bright pass, blur, add back.
World turning red, tinted shadowsColour grade in the same pass on non-sky pixels (depth gives the distance). Alternative: world_fragment of a world_warp.
Burgundy grass and leavesnative_biome_colors, swapped once mid-eclipse (a refresh rebuilds sections); the grade makes the change look continuous.
Red fog closing incompute_fog_color + render_fog.
Heartbeat pulse of red light near the playervisuals coloured lights (seen working on Cagebound), 8 per mod within 32 blocks; further away the grade carries it.
Shake at the climaxcamera_shake (≤ 10°, scaled by the player's Distortion Effects setting). The player keeps control.
Embers, glass glints, rising dustControlled particles (ParticleEngine::controlled), full bright.
Eye following the playerThe pass gets the camera matrices every frame; the pure core decides gaze, blink and pupil.
Heartbeat, drone, the crackmusic_layers + play_sound. The Sound button here is a rough synth stand-in, not the final audio.

To check in game (❓)

Long moon shadows: the preview casts them from a height map. Whether the engine casts moon shadows without a shader pack is not verified; fallback is screen-space shadows from the depth buffer in our pass.

Vanilla clouds are drawn before our pass: whether they write depth (and so cover our sky) is not verified. The preview has no clouds.

Not shown here

Nervous animals at sunset (gameplay), red light through windows, the golden dawn (event #11). The camera turn from the sunset to the moon at 0:11 is this preview's, not the game's.
